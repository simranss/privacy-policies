# Privacy Policy for Tic-Tac-Toe

**Effective date:** November 25, 2026
**Developer:** Simran Sharma ("we", "us", "our")
**Contact:** simran.s.sharma@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how Tic-Tac-Toe (the "App") handles information when you use it. By using the App you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

## 1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not directly collect your name, email address, or phone number through the App.

The App uses third-party services that may collect the following information automatically from your device:

- **Advertising identifiers** (such as the Google Advertising ID on Android or the IDFA on iOS)
- **IP address** and approximate location derived from it
- **Device information** such as device model, operating system version, language, and screen settings
- **App usage and interaction data**, such as ad impressions, clicks, and in-app events
- **Diagnostic data**, such as crash logs and performance information

If you enable it, the App may also store your game progress, settings, and scores locally on your device. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us.

## 2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads served by **Google AdMob**. AdMob may use the information listed above to show ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud, and, where you have given consent, personalize ads to your interests.

You can learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services here:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Google's Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 3. Consent for users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we ask for your consent before ad-related data is collected or used, using Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), a Google-certified consent management platform.

- You can accept, decline, or manage your choices when the consent form first appears.
- You can change or withdraw your consent at any time through the privacy settings option inside the App.
- If you decline personalized ads, you may still see ads, but they will not be tailored to your interests.

Our legal basis for processing this data is your **consent** (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). Where no consent is needed, we rely on our **legitimate interest** in operating and keeping the App free (Article 6(1)(f)).

## 4. Other ways to control advertising

- **Android:** Settings > Privacy > Ads, where you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization.
- **iOS:** Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, where you can disable tracking for the App.
- You can also visit https://www.youronlinechoices.com (EU) or https://optout.aboutads.info (US) to manage interest-based advertising.

## 5. How we use information

We use the information described above only to:

- Operate and improve the App
- Display advertising and measure its performance
- Detect and fix technical problems and abuse
- Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

## 6. Sharing of information

We do not share information with third parties except with service providers that help us run the App (such as Google AdMob), when required by law, or to protect our rights and the safety of users.

## 7. Data retention

We do not keep personal data on our own servers. Data collected by third-party services is retained according to their own policies. Locally stored game data is removed when you clear the App's data or uninstall it.

## 10. US state privacy rights (California and others)

This section applies to residents of US states with comprehensive privacy laws, such as California, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas, Virginia, and others.

**Categories of personal information.** In the past 12 months, the App may have collected, through our advertising partners: identifiers (such as advertising IDs and IP address), internet or app activity (such as ad interactions), device information, and approximate geolocation. We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information or precise geolocation.

**Sale, sharing, and targeted advertising.** We do not sell personal information for money. However, making advertising identifiers and related data available to ad partners such as Google AdMob for personalized advertising may count as a "sale", "sharing", or "targeted advertising" under some state laws.

**Your rights.** Depending on your state, you may have the right to:

- Know and access the personal information collected about you
- Delete or correct your personal information
- Opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information and of targeted advertising
- Not be discriminated against for exercising your rights

**How to opt out.** Where available, use the "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" or privacy settings option inside the App. We also honor opt-out preference signals, such as Global Privacy Control, where required by law. You can additionally reset or limit your device advertising ID in your device settings (see Section 4).

**How to make other requests.** Contact us at simran.s.sharma@gmail.com. We will respond within the time required by law (generally 45 days). We may need to verify your request. You may use an authorized agent to submit a request for you. If we deny a request, you may appeal by replying to our decision by email, and you may contact your state attorney general if you are unsatisfied with the outcome.

We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of consumers under 16.
